Our story

Seventeen years of Brisbane, street by street

Metro Real Estate Property Group has spent 17 years helping Brisbane owners sell, buy and manage property. As an independent, boutique agency, the team stays deliberately small — so the agent who appraises your home is the agent who presents it, negotiates for it and sees it through to settlement.

That continuity shapes everything: considered presentation for character Queenslanders and modern family homes alike, pricing advice grounded in local evidence rather than guesswork, and property management built on careful tenant selection and proactive maintenance. It is a slower, more personal way to do real estate — and for Brisbane sellers and landlords, it works.
